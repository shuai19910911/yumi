# ZEAMAP v0.1 Journal Strategy And Acceptance Estimate

日期：2026-06-05

## Current Manuscript Position

Current title:

```text
Prediction-guided mixed-model GWAS prioritizes maize oil-trait candidate loci in ZEAMAP
```

The current manuscript is a strong data-reanalysis and candidate-locus paper, not yet a high-impact functional genomics paper. Its strengths are reproducibility, accession-level harmonization, calibrated GEMMA LMM GWAS, oil-trait focus, clear candidate-locus boundaries and manuscript-ready figures/tables. Its main weakness is lack of independent validation, fine-mapping, expression evidence and experimental functional proof.

## Recommended Submission Tiers

### Tier A: Ambitious But High Risk

1. `Nature Plants`

Fit:

- Scope includes agriculture, genomics, metabolism and food-security-relevant plant biology.
- Best fit only if we add a stronger conceptual angle: ZEAMAP as a reusable accession-level prediction-to-GWAS framework plus biologically prioritized oil-trait loci.

Current acceptance estimate:

```text
Current skeleton: <2%
After Stage 5.9-5.10 polish and stronger annotation: 3-6%
With independent validation or functional evidence: 8-15%
```

Risk:

- The current paper is likely seen as careful secondary analysis rather than a broad conceptual or mechanistic advance.
- Nature-level journals will expect stronger novelty, broader biological insight or validation.

Recommendation:

- Do not submit here first unless we obtain independent validation, expression/functional support or a larger cross-dataset story.
- Could send a presubmission enquiry after strengthening the manuscript.

Official scope note:

- Nature Plants covers agriculture, genomics, metabolomics, metabolism, food security and related plant-science topics.
- Source: https://www.nature.com/nplants/aims

### Tier B: Strong Plant-Science Journals

2. `Journal of Experimental Botany`

Fit:

- Good fit if we frame the paper as crop molecular genetics/metabolism: prediction-guided oil-trait GWAS with candidate fatty-acid intervals.
- JXB states that original research should advance plant biology and includes crop molecular genetics, trait/gene characterization and metabolic processes.

Current acceptance estimate:

```text
Current skeleton: 5-10%
After Stage 5.9-5.10 polish: 12-22%
With stronger external annotation and one independent validation dataset: 20-35%
```

Risk:

- JXB may reject if the work looks mostly descriptive or if candidate genes remain unsupported.
- Need a stronger mechanistic interpretation around chr6 and chr9 while preserving candidate-language.

Recommendation:

- Good ambitious target after polishing, especially if Discussion is strengthened and Figure 2/3 are visually excellent.

Official scope note:

- JXB aims to publish work advancing plant biology, including mechanisms underpinning sustainable plant improvement; it lists crop molecular genetics, trait/gene characterization and metabolic processes.
- Sources: https://academic.oup.com/jxb/pages/About and https://academic.oup.com/jxb/pages/what-does-jxb-publish

3. `Plant Physiology`

Fit:

- Possible only if the manuscript emphasizes oil/fatty-acid metabolism and biological insight.
- Less ideal if the paper remains primarily benchmark + GWAS + candidate annotation.

Current acceptance estimate:

```text
Current skeleton: 3-8%
After major polish and stronger biological annotation: 8-15%
With functional/omics validation: 15-25%
```

Risk:

- Plant Physiology will likely expect deeper physiological or mechanistic evidence than the current v0.1 analysis provides.

Recommendation:

- Consider only after strengthening biological mechanism and annotation.

Official source used:

- Plant Physiology author guidance page: https://academic.oup.com/plphys/pages/General-Instructions

### Tier C: Best Fit For Current Evidence

4. `The Plant Genome`

Fit:

- Very good fit for a crop genomics, GWAS, genomic prediction and resource/analysis paper.
- Best match if we emphasize dataset harmonization, reproducible benchmark, calibrated oil-trait GWAS and candidate-locus resources.

Current acceptance estimate:

```text
Current skeleton: 15-25%
After Stage 5.9-5.10 polish: 30-45%
With stronger external annotation and full reproducibility package: 40-55%
```

Risk:

- Need to make the contribution more than "we reran GWAS"; the benchmark + candidate-locus framework must be clearly presented as reusable.

Recommendation:

- Strong recommended first real target if we want a realistic genomics journal.

Source note:

- Wiley page was not accessible from this environment due HTTP 403, but the journal is a Crop Science Society/Wiley plant genomics journal. We should verify current author guidelines manually before submission.

5. `G3: Genes|Genomes|Genetics`

Fit:

- Good fit for genetic/genomic research, GWAS, genomic prediction and agricultural-trait genomics.
- G3 explicitly states that it does not rely on subjective novelty/impact decisions and welcomes well-designed, executed and described genetics/genomics research.

Current acceptance estimate:

```text
Current skeleton: 20-35%
After Stage 5.9-5.10 polish: 40-60%
With full code/data reproducibility and stronger annotation: 50-70%
```

Risk:

- The manuscript must be methodologically transparent and avoid overclaiming candidate genes.

Recommendation:

- Very good pragmatic target if the priority is publication probability and reproducibility.

Official scope note:

- G3 welcomes well-designed genetics/genomics research and includes genomic prediction, GWAS, functional omics and agricultural trait genomics.
- Source: https://academic.oup.com/g3journal/pages/About

6. `BMC Plant Biology`

Fit:

- Strong fit for a scientifically valid plant genomics/crop biotechnology paper.
- BMC Plant Biology states that it does not decide based on perceived interest or impact if the research question, methods and analysis are sound.

Current acceptance estimate:

```text
Current skeleton: 25-40%
After Stage 5.9-5.10 polish: 45-65%
With complete reproducibility and external annotation: 55-75%
```

Risk:

- Needs a clear biological story and clean methods. It may be less selective on novelty but still requires methodological soundness.

Recommendation:

- Best high-probability target if we want a robust publication path.

Official scope note:

- BMC Plant Biology covers plant biology broadly, including genetics/crop biotechnology and genomics/evolution, and considers scientifically valid research without perceived-impact gating.
- Source: https://bmcplantbiol.biomedcentral.com/submission-guidelines/aims-and-scope
